# Checklist

Before delivering anything for TDSA:

1. Read this design system before choosing any colour or font.
2. The logo is a real asset file — never redrawn — and the right variant for its background (`-positive` black on light, `-white` on dark).
3. Only brand hex values are used (the seven brand colours plus white).
4. Text contrast passes 4.5:1 (see Contrast rules in the README).
5. Avenir, or the fallback stack, is applied throughout.
6. Photography is real Saudi aerial or industrial imagery, not illustration.
7. The page or deck opens light and closes on a dark Night band.
8. Buttons are short and verb-led, with one primary style and one outline style.
9. Layouts leave room for an Arabic (RTL) version.
10. No purple gradients, emoji decoration or off-brand accent colours.
