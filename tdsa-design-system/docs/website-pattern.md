# Website pattern

How terra-drone.com.sa applies the brand. Use this flow as the model for web pages, landing pages and long-form decks.

1. **Header** — light background (`surface`), positive long logo (`tdsa-logo-long-positive.png`) on the left, nav: Home, About, Services, Resources, Contact, plus an Arabic language switch (العربية).
2. **Hero** — short, punchy three-beat headline ("Every Terrain. Every Angle. One Partner.") in `display`, two CTAs: primary "Contact Us", secondary "Schedule Meeting", over or beside real drone/product photography.
3. **Proof strips** — technology-partner logos; then client logos (Saudi Arabia, then Global Network); then certifications (GACA, GEOSA, ISO 9001 / 22301 / 45001, Vision 2030 badge). Clean rows on light grounds.
4. **Value section** — "Why Industry Leaders Choose Us".
5. **Service cards** — Drone Services and Geospatial Services, each with a photo and a "View All …" link.
6. **Industries, case studies, insights** — photo-led cards with "Learn More" / "Full Case Study" / "Read More" links.
7. **Closing CTA band** — `surface-band` (night) with a confident two-part line: "Data you can trust, Operations you can scale".
8. **Footer** — dark (`night`), white logo (`tdsa-logo-long-white.png`), office addresses (Riyadh, Dhahran), newsletter signup over a Riyadh skyline image.

For decks, the same arc: open on a light title slide with photography, proof slides in the middle, close on a Night slide with the two-part line and contact details.
