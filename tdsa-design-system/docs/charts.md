# Charts

- **Series order:** `chart-1` FineDay → `chart-2` Dusk → `chart-3` Twilight → `chart-4` Dawn → `chart-5` Night.
- **Gridlines** in `chart-grid` (Sunset); **labels and axes** in `chart-label` (Night), Avenir Medium or Book.
- FineDay and Twilight are nearly identical in lightness (1.04:1). With three or more series, label lines and bars directly or add markers — never rely on colour alone.
- Charts sit on `surface` or `surface-alt`; no 3D, no gradients, no drop shadows.
- Use a single series in `chart-2` (Dusk) when one colour is enough, with `chart-1` for the highlighted value.
